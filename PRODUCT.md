# Polysyne

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, SMEs, growing companies, service businesses, and potential partners evaluating Polysyne and its products.

## Product Purpose

A corporate marketing website establishing Polysyne as a software technology company, introducing Opero, and collecting business inquiries and early-access interest.

## Positioning

Polysyne builds practical, adaptable business technology. Opero is its flagship multi-tenant business operations platform, actively under development. The company identity must extend beyond one product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five routes: Home, Products, Opero, About, Contact. Dashboard data and product interfaces are explicitly conceptual. Intended capabilities and target sectors are not deployed-feature claims. The SaaS application remains separate. Forms must not simulate acceptance; server validation and a future FastAPI integration are required.

## Brand Commitments

User-supplied dark-first palette, Geist typography, thoughtful software engineering, minimal modern composition, accessible restrained motion. Linear, Vercel, Stripe, and Raycast are craft references. No copied layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's marketing brief supplies company positioning and approved copy. No verified customers, production metrics, testimonials, certifications, biographies, company email, domain, or social accounts have been supplied.

## Product Principles

Simplify complexity; design for scale; engineer with purpose; distinguish ambition from verified functionality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive mobile/tablet/desktop layouts, keyboard-complete navigation and forms, visible focus, semantic structure, and reduced-motion support.

## Open Configuration

Public domain, contact details, leadership, social links, lead endpoint, and analytics provider remain configurable. Their absence must never create invented information or fake success.
